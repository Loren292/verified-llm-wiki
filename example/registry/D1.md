# D1 · Refunded orders out of the metrics

**What:** orders, revenue and AOV count only `paid` orders.

**Why:** a refunded order isn't revenue. With refunds in, revenue and AOV were overstated: 3 of
the 12 orders were refunded ([[H1]]).

**How:** recomputed from `raw/orders.csv`. [[metrics]] updated, with the hash of its source.

**Where else this is calculated:** nowhere else. Checked with `wikicheck extract AOV revenue`:
the only definitions are in [[metrics]].

**Validation:**

| Check | Expected | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Paid orders | 9 | 9 |
| Revenue: sum of `amount` over the 9 paid rows | $374.60 | $374.60 |
| AOV: revenue / 9, two decimals | $41.62 | $41.62 |

Before D1, with all 12 orders: revenue $516.50 and AOV $43.04 (old).
